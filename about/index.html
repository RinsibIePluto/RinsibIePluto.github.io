About Cyrune

Cyrune is a daily code-breaking word puzzle. Every day, one five-letter word is hidden behind a classic cipher, the same puzzle for every player, and you have six guesses to crack it.

How a puzzle works

Each day's cipher is picked at random from 17, from a simple Caesar shift to a working Enigma machine. You guess words like in other word games, and the coloured tiles tell you which letters are right. Every correct letter is also a clue to the key: turn the rotors, read the decode, and crack the cipher before your guesses run out.

Why ciphers?

Ciphers are some of the best puzzles ever made, and many of them changed history: the codes of Julius Caesar and Mary, Queen of Scots, the Playfair messages of the First World War, and the Enigma machine broken at Bletchley Park. Cyrune lets you try them for yourself, a few minutes a day. If you want the background, the cipher guides explain how each one works and how it's cracked.

Where to play

You can play Cyrune in your browser on any phone or computer; on an iPhone, add it to your home screen from Safari's Share menu. An Android app is coming to Google Play.

Who makes it
