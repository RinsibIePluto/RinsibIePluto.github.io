Cyrune privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026

In short: Cyrune has no accounts and keeps your games, stats and settings on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service collects to show ads, and the basic visit logs kept by our website host.

Who we are

Cyrune is a daily cipher puzzle game made by an independent developer, RinsibIePluto ("we"). This policy covers the Cyrune Android app (package com.rinsibiepluto.cyrune), the browser version at cyrune.app/play, and the rest of the cyrune.app website.

What stays on your phone

Cyrune stores the following only on your device. We never receive it.

  • Your puzzles in progress, past results, streaks and statistics
  • Your settings, such as sound, vibration, hard mode, colour-blind mode and reminder time
  • Daily reminders, which are scheduled by your phone itself if you turn them on

Uninstalling Cyrune, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it. In the browser version, the same information is kept in your browser's local storage for cyrune.app; clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Cyrune makes a picture of your result or a challenge code and passes it to the Android share sheet. It goes only to the app and people you choose.

Ads

Cyrune is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, that SDK collects:

  • Device identifiers, including your Android advertising ID
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • How you interact with ads and the app, such as when an ad is shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic information, such as crashes and performance

Google processes this data under its own policies; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. The data is encrypted in transit.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Cyrune asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Menu → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most phones).

The website

cyrune.app is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub records the IP address of visitors in its logs for security and to keep the service running; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The only outside script the website loads is Google's AdSense ad code; it uses no other outside scripts or fonts, and we don't run our own analytics.

cyrune.app shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, based on your visits to this and other websites. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent first, and anyone can turn off personalised ads at Google's Ad Settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any other account details.
  • We don't run our own servers or analytics, and we don't sell data.
  • The Cyrune app requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone or file permissions.

Children

Cyrune is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
